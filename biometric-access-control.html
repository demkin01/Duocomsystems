Biometric Access Control FAQs

Common questions about biometric security systems

How accurate are biometric systems?

Modern biometric systems have accuracy rates exceeding 99%. False acceptance rates are extremely low, and advanced anti-spoofing technology prevents unauthorized access attempts.

Can biometric data be stolen or hacked?

Biometric templates are stored as encrypted mathematical representations, not actual images. Even if data is compromised, it cannot be reverse-engineered to recreate fingerprints or facial features.

What happens during power outages?

Our systems include battery backup that can last several hours. For critical access points, we recommend UPS systems for extended power outage protection.

How many users can the system support?

Our systems can support from 100 to 50,000+ users depending on the configuration. We'll recommend the right system based on your user count and scalability needs.

Can biometric systems integrate with existing security?

Yes, our biometric systems can integrate with CCTV, alarm systems, and other security infrastructure to create a comprehensive security ecosystem.
