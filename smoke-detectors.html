Safety Systems FAQs

Common questions about smoke detectors and safety systems

How often should smoke detectors be tested?

Smoke detectors should be tested monthly by pressing the test button. We recommend professional testing and maintenance every six months to ensure optimal performance.

What's the difference between photoelectric and ionization detectors?

Photoelectric detectors are better at detecting smoldering fires, while ionization detectors are more responsive to flaming fires. We recommend using both types or dual-sensor detectors for comprehensive protection.

How long do smoke detectors last?

Most smoke detectors should be replaced every 10 years. We put expiration dates on our installations and provide replacement reminders as part of our maintenance services.

Can smoke detectors be interconnected wirelessly?

Yes, we offer both wired and wireless interconnected systems. Wireless systems are ideal for existing buildings where running new wires would be difficult.

Do you install systems for people with hearing impairments?

Yes, we specialize in systems for hearing-impaired individuals, including strobe light alarms, bed shakers, and specialized alert systems that meet accessibility requirements.
