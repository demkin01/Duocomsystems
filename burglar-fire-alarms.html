Alarm Systems FAQs

Common questions about burglar and fire alarm systems

How do false alarms get prevented?

Our systems include advanced false alarm reduction features such as cross-zone verification, pet-immune motion sensors, and entry/exit delays. Professional monitoring also includes verification calls before dispatching emergency services.

What happens during a power outage?

All our alarm systems include battery backup that can last 24-48 hours. For extended protection, we recommend UPS systems for critical applications.

Can I control my alarm system remotely?

Yes, our modern alarm systems include mobile app control, allowing you to arm/disarm your system, receive alerts, and view system status from anywhere.

How often should alarm systems be tested?

We recommend monthly testing of all sensors and quarterly professional maintenance. Our maintenance plans include regular testing and system health checks.

Can alarm systems integrate with other security systems?

Yes, our alarm systems can integrate with CCTV, access control, and smart home systems to create a comprehensive security ecosystem.
