Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can you install a security system?

For standard installations, we can typically complete the setup within 1-3 business days after your consultation. Emergency installations for urgent security needs can be arranged within 24 hours.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance packages for all our security systems. Our maintenance plans include regular check-ups, software updates, and 24/7 technical support to ensure your systems are always functioning optimally.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Nairobi and its surrounding areas, with our main office located in Roysambu. We cover all neighborhoods in Nairobi and can arrange installations in neighboring counties for larger projects.

Do you provide warranties on your installations?

Yes, all our installations come with a comprehensive warranty. We offer a 2-year warranty on workmanship and a 1-year warranty on equipment. Extended warranty options are available for commercial installations.

Can I integrate existing security systems with your solutions?

In most cases, yes. We can assess your existing security infrastructure and integrate it with our solutions where compatible. During our free consultation, we'll evaluate your current setup and provide recommendations.
