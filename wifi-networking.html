WiFi Networking FAQs

Common questions about WiFi extenders and networking solutions

What's the difference between WiFi extenders and mesh systems?

WiFi extenders rebroadcast your existing signal, while mesh systems create a seamless network with multiple access points working together. Mesh systems typically provide better performance and easier management for larger properties.

How many devices can your networks support?

Our commercial-grade systems can support 100+ devices simultaneously, while residential systems typically handle 30-50 devices depending on the configuration and internet speed.

Do you provide equipment or should I purchase my own?

We can provide professional-grade equipment as part of our service package, or we can work with your existing equipment. We'll recommend the best solution during our free consultation.

How long does a typical WiFi installation take?

Most residential installations take 2-4 hours, while larger commercial installations may take 1-2 days depending on the size and complexity of the property.

Can you integrate with existing network infrastructure?

Yes, we can integrate with most existing network equipment and often optimize existing setups to improve performance before considering additional hardware.
