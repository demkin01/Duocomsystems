Protecting What Matters Most Since 2018

For over a decade, Duocomsystems has been the trusted partner for security and telecom solutions in Nairobi and beyond. Our commitment to excellence and innovation has secured thousands of homes and businesses.

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Duocomsystems began as a small security installation company in Roysambu, Nairobi. Our founder, Royford Kirimi & Amo Kirimi, recognized the growing need for reliable security solutions in the rapidly developing community.

What started with basic CCTV installations has evolved into a comprehensive security and telecom provider serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Nairobi and surrounding areas.

Over the years, we've grown from a one-person operation to a team of certified security experts, but our core philosophy remains the same: provide exceptional service, use quality equipment, and build lasting relationships with our clients.

Today, we're proud to have secured over 100 properties and maintained a 98% customer satisfaction rate through our commitment to excellence and continuous innovation.
