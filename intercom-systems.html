Intercom Systems FAQs

Common questions about intercom and door phone systems

Can intercom systems work without internet?

Yes, traditional wired intercom systems operate independently of internet. However, modern systems with mobile app integration require internet for remote features.

How many units can an intercom system support?

Our systems can support from 2 units up to 500+ units depending on the configuration. We'll design a system that meets your specific capacity requirements.

Can intercom systems integrate with other security systems?

Yes, our intercom systems can integrate with CCTV, access control, and alarm systems to create a comprehensive security ecosystem.

What's the difference between audio and video intercoms?

Audio intercoms provide voice communication only, while video intercoms include cameras for visual identification of visitors, providing enhanced security.

How long does intercom installation typically take?

Single-unit installations take 2-4 hours, while multi-unit systems for apartment buildings may take 2-5 days depending on the size and complexity.
