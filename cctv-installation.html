CCTV Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about CCTV installation

How long does CCTV installation take?

Most residential installations can be completed within 1-2 days, while larger commercial systems may take 3-5 days depending on the complexity and size of the property.

Can I view my CCTV cameras remotely?

Yes, all our modern CCTV systems include remote viewing capabilities through mobile apps or web browsers, allowing you to monitor your property from anywhere with an internet connection.

Do you offer maintenance services after installation?

Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance plans that include regular system check-ups, software updates, and 24/7 technical support to ensure your CCTV system remains operational at all times.

What areas do you serve for CCTV installation?

We primarily serve Nairobi and surrounding areas, with our main office located in Roysambu. We cover all neighborhoods in Nairobi and can arrange installations in neighboring counties for larger projects.

Are your CCTV systems compatible with existing security systems?

In most cases, yes. We can assess your existing security infrastructure during our free consultation and integrate our CCTV systems where compatible, or recommend upgrades if needed.
